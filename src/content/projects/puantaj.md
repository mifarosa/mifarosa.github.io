A scoreboard for two teams that fills the phone screen: each half is one team, tap the upper half to add a point and the lower half to take one away. Numbers change with a split-flap animation like an old stadium board. A strip in the middle holds settings, undo, set or period info, the match clock and a side swap.

Knows the rules of table tennis (serve rotation, deuce at 10–10), volleyball (deciding set to 15), basketball (+1/+2/+3 with a countdown clock) and football (match clock with stoppage time), plus a plain A–B counter. Keeps a match history, survives reloads, works offline and installs on Android and iOS. No dependencies and no build step.
