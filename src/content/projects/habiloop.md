A habit tracker for things that don't happen every day, like cleaning the litter box or watering the plants. Instead of a daily streak, each habit has a window: choose every how many days and a tolerance, and Habiloop works out the ideal day and the last day from when you last did it. Habits are sorted by urgency and move from relaxed to ideal day, last day and overdue; when the day comes, it asks whether you did it.

Progress is shown as an on-time rate rather than a streak, and after a few entries it suggests a better interval if your real rhythm differs. A calendar lets you edit any past day, and there are reminders with a "Done" button on the notification itself. Data stays on the device unless you turn on the optional Google sync, which encrypts habits in the browser before they leave it. Works offline, installs on Android and iOS, and comes in English and Turkish with light and dark themes.
