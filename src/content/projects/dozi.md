Tracks a medicine box the way it really is: enter how many blisters it has and how many pills each holds, then start from where you are, even mid-blister or with half a pill left. Remaining pills show as blister cells, together with an estimated end date.

Supports quarter, half, one, one and a half and two-pill doses several times a day, a "Taken" button with undo, and up to three reminder times per medicine that notify you when a dose is overdue. Data stays on the device; it works offline and installs on Android and iOS. Turkish interface, no build step.
