Tracks a medicine box the way it really is: enter how many blisters it has and how many pills each holds, then start from where you are, even mid-blister or with half a pill left. Remaining pills show as blister cells, together with an estimated end date.

Supports quarter, half, one, one and a half and two-pill doses several times a day, a "Taken" button with undo, and up to three reminder times per medicine that notify you when a dose is overdue. Also handles forgotten doses, prompts for a new box when one runs out, and shows a time-of-day chart, a calendar by week, a free-form drink log and a settings tab with theme, accent colour and data download.

Reminders can also arrive as server push notifications from a small home-server notifier, set up with a one-command installer. Data stays on the device by default, with optional Google sign-in backup through Firebase. Works offline and installs on Android and iOS. Turkish interface.
