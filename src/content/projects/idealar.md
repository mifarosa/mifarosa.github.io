A notebook for app ideas that thinks along with you. Write whatever comes to mind and the AI fills in a title, a summary, tags and feature suggestions. Then it reads all your other notes, projects and tags and tells you what connects: related notes, duplicates worth merging, features worth borrowing, the project it belongs to and new ideas that only appear when notes are combined. Nothing is applied until you confirm it.

Ideas can be linked to projects, imported from GitHub, checked off feature by feature and turned into a ready-to-paste build prompt for a coding assistant. There is a chat for talking an idea or a whole project through, and kanban boards: one for each project, where its ideas become cards, and a personal one for everything else.

You bring your own AI: add a key for Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Groq, Mistral and more, stored encrypted and never shown again. Sign in with Google, download or delete all your data at any time. Installs as an app on Android and iOS, works in English and Turkish, and has light, gray and dark themes.
