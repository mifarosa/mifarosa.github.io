Interval timer for a home exercise bike, built to run alongside music from another app. Pick a 30, 40, 45, 50 or 60 minute flow: an easy warm-up, steady moderate blocks, then one-minute moderate and hard intervals, and an easy cool-down. The screen changes colour with each level, and short beeps count down every change without pausing the music. Optional Turkish voice cues, vibration, and the screen stays on for the whole session.

A ten-second warning comes before each change, each level shows a bike target, you rate the effort at the end, and an interrupted session can be resumed. The time of day sits on the workout screen, and the runner has a landscape layout.

Also includes fat-burning circuits using bodyweight and a single 7.5 kg dumbbell, with exercise photos and how-to notes. Build your own plans from sections, picking each step by equipment, and count the weight moves in reps.

Progress shows up in a calendar with weekly streaks and calorie estimates, plus a goals page with 30-day challenges (squats, push-ups, sit-ups, plank) and your own monthly totals. Optional Google sign-in backs up history, plans and goals to Firebase and syncs them between devices, while the app stays local first. Installable on Android and iOS and works offline. No build step.
