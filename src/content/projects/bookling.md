A reader for language learners: import an EPUB or TXT book, or paste an article, and every word is colour-coded by how well you know it. Tap a word to see a translation, look it up in your own dictionaries, hear it pronounced and set its status from new to known. Marking the page as read moves on, like LingQ.

Works with any language, including Japanese, Chinese and right-to-left scripts. Remembers your reading position and notes, shows weekly progress, and imports and exports vocabulary in Lute's CSV format. Everything stays on the device in IndexedDB, with no account and no server; it installs on Android and iOS and works offline.
