Pour coloured water between glass tubes until each tube holds a single colour. Tap a tube to pick it up, then tap another to pour. Liquid only goes into an empty tube or onto the same colour.

There are 300 levels in nine stages. Each stage teaches a new rule: one-way tubes, tall tubes, hidden colours, cracked tubes, locked tubes, half tubes and oil. The last 40 levels mix the rules, and finishing them opens a surprise. A solver checks every level before you see it, so each one can be solved, and it counts the fewest moves for your stars. A daily puzzle with its own rules is the same for everyone, with a daily leaderboard when you sign in. You can also make your own puzzles in the workshop and share them with a code. Undo, restart and hints are there when you get stuck. It is drawn on a plain canvas, so it stays light and smooth on phones, and it can be installed as an app. English and Turkish.
